2.1 DeepSeekSparseAttention
DeepSeekSparseAttention(DSA)(Liuetal.,2025)decomposeseachattentionlayerintotwo
stages: selectionandcomputation. Alightweightlightningindexerfirstscoresallpreceding

subset,reducingper-layercoreattentionfromO(L2)toO(Lk)withk=2048≪ L,whereL

DSAisinstantiatedunderMLAandintroducedthroughtwo-stagedcontinuepre-training.
First,ashortdensewarm-uptrainsonlytheindexerviaKL-divergencedistillationagainstthe
aggregatedfullattentiondistributionateachlayer,whileallotherparametersarefrozen.
Then,alongersparsetrainingphaseactivatestop-kselectionandjointlyoptimizestheentire
model,withtheindexerreceivingdistillationgradientsonadetachedcomputationalgraph.
Despitetheseefficiencygains,theindexeritselfstilloperatesatO(L2):ateverylayer,itmust
independentlyscoreallprecedingtokenstodetermineitsowntop-kset. Acrossamodel

layerscanbeexploited.

A Cross-LayerTop-k IndexOverlap
Toempiricallyvalidatethecross-layerredundancyoftop-k indexselectionsinDSA,we
compute the pairwise overlap ratio between the top-k indices selected by each layer's
