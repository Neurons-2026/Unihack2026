5 Conclusion
Inthispaper,wepresentSpatial-TTT,anovelframeworkforstreamingvisual-basedspatial
intelligencethatleveragestest-timetrainingtomaintainadaptivefastweightsascompact

pretrainedknowledgewhileenablingefficientlong-contextprocessing,furtherenhanced

inductivebias,andconstructadensescene-descriptiondatasettoproviderichsupervision

memory,enablingmorerobustandscalablespatialintelligenceinreal-worldapplications.
